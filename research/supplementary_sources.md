# Supplementary sources (same author) — used only to define the video's own terms

The target video (V) uses the author's vocabulary without defining it: *middle time frame range*, *trending range*,
*overextend*, *type three shift*, *candle behavior*, *shift within the shift*. Guessing those definitions would mean
replacing the author's method with generic SMC/ICT definitions, which the brief forbids. So the definitions were taken
from the author's **own** public videos on the same channel. Captions were fetched the same way as for V
(`yt-dlp`, `web_embedded` client, auto-generated English captions). Only short quotes are reproduced.

**Precedence rule used throughout the project:** V > S-sources (same author) > X-sources (third parties).
An S-source is used to *define* a term or to *quantify* a vague word in V. It never overrides V.

## Key definitions extracted (short quotes with timestamps)

### Type three shift (used in V 02:00, 09:42 without definition)
- S4 05:32: "What a type three shift looks like, it's just **a break of a low into a break of a high**. A shift in highs and
  lows … For a bearish example … we're going to **break a high into immediately breaking a low**."
- S2 240:36 (entry model): "where we go from price action **taking out a previous high into immediately taking out a
  previous low**" — "we specifically want … a **high volume impulse**"; 242:04 "a bullish type three shift … where we take
  out a low into then immediately taking out a high, back into 50% then continuing."
- S2 282:17: "you're not just looking for it to like barely take out this low … you want it to have a **clear take out**."
- S2 242:47: no entries on a mere change of character ("could just be a pullback").
- Type one / type two: **not defined** in any source we could read (S8 13:00 uses "15-minute type one" for a candle that
  closes in your favour and then wicks first; S4 10:04 and S5 04:31 mention a "type two shift" in passing). They are not
  needed for V.

### Middle time frame (MTF), condition, trending range
- S6 01:22: "lower time frame … the past 15, 20 minutes … **Middle time frame is the past 4 to 5 hours** … High time frame
  … the past days." (S2 271:44 says 5–12 h; S3 08:40 accepts "5 to 8-plus hours".)
- S2 218:28: "direction is defined by highs and lows. **Condition is defined by the size of the pullbacks**."
- S2 220:15–222:27: range ≈ pullbacks **75–100%** or more; trending range ≈ **50–75%** with clear highs and lows in one
  direction; trend ≈ **under 50%**.
- S4 03:07: "correcting under 50% … you're in a trend … around 50% … a trending range … over 50% … a range."
- S4 02:46: "**If it's trending, I'm not trading.**" S2 104:47: "If it's in a trend, I do not trade."
- S2 223:49: "the direction in a range isn't sideways it's **towards the midpoint** of that range."

### Location (entry relativity, premium/discount)
- S3 02:04: "in a bullish trading range … buys in the **lower half of the previous bullish move** … in a bearish trading
  range … sells in the **upper half of the previous bearish move**."
- S2 229:06: trend → enter ≈50% or less; trending range → 50–75%; range → 75–100%.
- S2 234:03 (author's journal claim): buys in the lower half / sells in the upper half ≈ 89% win; the opposite ≈ 40%.

### Overextension
- S1 01:04–01:24: "overextend with high volume without much of a pullback … We want price to just move in one direction
  without a pullback at all … you want this **extension to last for around 20 minutes**."
- S1 06:19: "at least 20 minutes of price extending in one direction without a pullback … **I wouldn't go any lower than
  18**." S1 07:45: "at least 20 minutes of price moving in one direction **without a pullback to 50%**."
- S4 04:50: "an overextension is … at least **15 to 30 minutes** of price … push in one direction."
- S1 05:57: a late extension is acceptable ("delayed entry"), still ≥ 20 min.

### Timing
- S2 104:47 (daily checklist): "Does price have a type three shift **22 to 52 minutes into the hour**? No, I won't trade."
  S2 102:18: "What time is it, 22, 37, 52 minutes into the hour?" (= the half-way points of the 2nd–4th 15-minute candles).
- S1 02:07: "the shift to happen around the halfway point, around **30 to 45 minutes** within this hourly candle."
- S2 260:38: "Reversals around 30 to 45 minutes into the hour." S5 07:20: "around 15 or 30 minutes into an hourly candle."

### Entry, stop, target
- S1 02:50: "enter at around **50% of the breaking move** … stop above the swing high … target **50% of the hourly candle
  extension** … draw out from the swing low to the swing high" (06:41: "including the wicks").
- S4 13:40: "you pretty much always want to wait for a pullback towards [50]% of a type three shift … **If it doesn't pull
  back, you miss the trade.** … I miss like around 30% of my trades."
- S4 06:56: "For stop-loss, you're just doing the high that you broke or the low that you broke."
- S5 09:48: on a very small (seconds-chart) shift "I just look to **enter at the breakout**."
- S5 07:00: "I tend to give my stop loss a bit of breathing room … I always account for spread."
- S3 10:04: on the pullback, "I wait for a previous 1-minute high to be broken for buys."
