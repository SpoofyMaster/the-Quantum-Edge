Events (closed bars)

1 · Displacement candle

Body larger than the average body of the last Length candles, and each wick shorter than 36 % of the body.

body > sma(body, len)
high − max(o,c) < 0.36·body
min(o,c) − low  < 0.36·body

2 · Fair value gap

The candle after a bullish displacement leaves a gap above the candle before it. The box spans the gap. Bearish is the mirror image.

bull: low[0] > high[2]  → box high[2] … low[0]
bear: high[0] < low[2]  → box high[0] … low[2]

3 · Balance price range

On every bar, the newest bullish and newest bearish gap are compared. If one gap's bottom sits inside the other gap, a BPR box is drawn.

green: dn.bot < up.bot < dn.top → up.bot … dn.top
red:   up.bot < dn.bot < up.top → dn.bot … up.top

4 · Support, resistance, break

Close above the box bottom on the creation bar makes it support (breaks when a low trades below the bottom). Otherwise it is resistance (breaks when a high trades above the top). Active boxes extend 8 bars to the right.

pos +1: low < top → dashed; low < bottom → broken
pos −1: high > bottom → dashed; high > top → broken
