## How the words were read (choices left open are inputs or documented)
**Reversal setup** (`InpSetupType = REVERSAL`, or `BOTH` for either kind).
- **Touches.** T1 / T2 are unchanged.
- **Reversal level.** The low (bullish BPR) / high (bearish BPR) of **the candle that made the latest touch**. The
  owner's words; a T2 replaces the T1 level.
- **Breakout.** A **close** beyond that level, then the next close(s) beyond it (`InpConfirmCloses`, 2). "Close with
  the body below" = the close, as in v2's continuation rule. The candles may be inside the zone, since they cross it.
- **Leg.** From the touch's opposite extreme to the new extreme: for a bullish BPR, the highest high since the touch
  began down to the lowest low after it. It is tracked like the continuation leg.
- **Entries.** The same pullback limits ("same logic"), with the FVG of the trade direction.
- **A broken BPR** only ends the continuation (with REVERSAL / BOTH); it is the reversal's path.
- **The direction filter** applies to the trade.

**Touch entry** (`InpTouchEntry`).
- **"Market order directly after T1".** When the T1 candle closes, a market order at the next open (no look-ahead),
  in the BPR's direction: the "initial reaction".
- **Not specified, so chosen:**
  - the stop goes beyond the BPR's far edge + `InpStopBufferTicks`;
  - the take-profit is `InpTouchTargetRR` × the risk (1.5);
  - only T1;
  - not when the T1 candle broke the BPR;
  - sent once or never.
- **The setup continues** to its own breakout and limits. The setup's 0.20 % is split over the entries plus the
  touch entry.

**Research status.** Registered **H-15** (reversal) and **H-16** (touch entry) as `HYPOTHESIS`, untested.

## Adversarial review of v6 and fixes
A review of the v6 diff (detector and executor read separately, each finding checked by an independent skeptic)
confirmed 11 findings. All are fixed in the next commit.

**Detector (3).**
- **The failing candle.** A `BREAK_FAIL` candle that closes beyond the other kind's level now starts that breakout on
  the same bar (with `BOTH`). Before, a bull trap (continuation fails on a close below the touch candle's low) lost the
  reversal, because the candle was neither a touch nor tested again. New sequences `cont_fail_then_rev` and
  `rev_fail_then_cont_bar`.
- **A broken BPR with no possible reversal.** `contOff` is set only when the reversal's direction is allowed;
  otherwise `DONE(BROKEN)` as in v5. Before, e.g. `BOTH` + `LONG_ONLY` on a bullish BPR kept a dead setup open.
- **Kept BPRs.** The creation filter keeps a BPR with `InpTouchEntry` and an allowed BPR direction, so
  `REVERSAL` + `LONG_ONLY` still gets its T1 buys on bullish BPRs. The reversal level's candle (`lvlRBar`) is stored
  for the setups CSV and the drawing.

**Executor (8).**
- **Position id.** The touch position falls back to the order ticket when the deal is not yet in the history.
  `PosIdOfMarketFill` itself is unchanged: a level's netting merge needs its 0 fallback.
- **Order of management.** `ManageTouch` runs before `Reconcile`, so the touch position is never adopted as an
  unowned position.
- **Fill check.** `CheckTouchFillRisk`: like a market-filled level, slippage beyond the touch entry's share is closed
  (the excess on hedging, the whole position on netting).
- **No overlap.** `ExecTouch` refuses while `g_tp` is still active or not yet written to the CSV.
- **Exit reasons.** `TIME` / `ROLLOVER` / `RISK` reach the trades CSV (was `MANUAL`).
- **CSV.** Setups gain `bpr_dir, setup`; trades gain `setup` (`CONT` / `REV` / `TOUCH`). The files are renamed
  `BFEA6_*` so that v5 files with fewer columns are not appended to. The rows written in `OnDeinit` are rev-aware.
- **Drawing.** The breakout line starts at the reversal candle (`lvlBar`); the `BUY/SELL MKT` label turns grey with
  "not sent" when the order is not sent.

**Tests added (7):** the failing-candle hand-over both ways and the broken BPR whose reversal is filtered out (each
long and short), and the touch-entry keep filter (`REVERSAL` + `LONG_ONLY`).

## Second check (the fix diff, 763b1dc..d85d0c3)
Two reviewers (executor, detector) read the fix diff; each finding went to an independent skeptic. Detector: no
findings. Executor: 2 confirmed, both minor, both fixed in the next commit.
- **A refused partial close could hold back the time stop.** `CheckTouchFillRisk` (new in d85d0c3) ran before the
  time stop / 16:44 flat in `ManageTouch`, and they share one retry timer. On a symbol whose minimum volume is above
  its step, a cut below the minimum is refused on every retry, so the touch position would have been held past
  120 minutes. Fixes:
  - `ManageTouch` now checks the time stop and the flat first, as `ManagePositions` does;
  - both fill checks (touch and level) cut at least the minimum volume, else close the whole position.

  On IC Markets FX / metals the minimum equals the step (0.01), so it could not happen there.
- **The daily-loss wait message** printed the levels' planned risk only, not the touch position's risk that the check
  also counted. It now prints both.

After the fixes: the same command gives **404 passed in 11.57 s**; the static check prints `possibly undefined: []`
and `balance ok`. These lines are executor code, outside the harness: checked by reading only.
