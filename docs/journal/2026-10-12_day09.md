# Day 09 — 2026-10-12 — BprFvgEA v6: a reversal setup and a market entry on T1

## Request (owner)
> "now i want to add on the input the possibility to make this EA opening a Market order directly after T1, making
> the EA anticipating the initial reaction of price when he touch the BPR.
> also i want you to add another type of BPR price reaction trading, this time is the reversal setup, we keep T1 and
> T2 on the BPR as first step but the second one will be a breakout on the opposite direction, price must comes back
> and close below or above the high or low of the Candle that makes the T1 or T2, after that of couse the breakout must
> be done by minimum 2 successive candles that must close with the body below the low of the T1 or T2 candle when
> price goes down on the breakout or with the body above the high of the T1 or T2 candle when price goes up on the
> breakout. for the entry is the same logic of following price and place limit orders on pullback"

## How the words were read (choices left open are inputs or documented)
**Reversal setup** (`InpSetupType = REVERSAL`, or `BOTH` for either kind).
- **Touches.** T1 / T2 are unchanged.
- **Reversal level.** The low (bullish BPR) / high (bearish BPR) of **the candle that made the latest touch**. The
  owner's words; a T2 replaces the T1 level.
- **Breakout.** A **close** beyond that level, then the next close(s) beyond it (`InpConfirmCloses`, 2). "Close with
  the body below" = the close, as in v2's continuation rule. The candles may be inside the zone, since they cross it.
- **Leg.** From the touch's opposite extreme to the new extreme: for a bullish BPR, the highest high since the touch
  began down to the lowest low after it. It is tracked like the continuation leg.
- **Entries.** The same pullback limits ("same logic"), with the FVG of the trade direction.
- **A broken BPR** only ends the continuation (with REVERSAL / BOTH); it is the reversal's path.
- **The direction filter** applies to the trade.

**Touch entry** (`InpTouchEntry`).
- **"Market order directly after T1".** When the T1 candle closes, a market order at the next open (no look-ahead),
  in the BPR's direction: the "initial reaction".
- **Not specified, so chosen:**
  - the stop goes beyond the BPR's far edge + `InpStopBufferTicks`;
  - the take-profit is `InpTouchTargetRR` × the risk (1.5);
  - only T1;
  - not when the T1 candle broke the BPR;
  - sent once or never.
- **The setup continues** to its own breakout and limits. The setup's 0.20 % is split over the entries plus the
  touch entry.

**Research status.** Registered **H-15** (reversal) and **H-16** (touch entry) as `HYPOTHESIS`, untested.

## Work done (MQL5 only, like v5)
**Detector** (`include/BfDefines.mqh`, `include/BfDetector.mqh`).
- **New inputs:** `setupType`, `touchEntry`, `touchTargetRR`.
- **New setup fields:** `zdir`, `rev`, `lvlR`, `rO` / `rX`, `contOff`, `touchReady`, `tP` / `tSL` / `tTP`.
- **Event and intent.** `TOUCH_ENTRY` (13, level 0). The event snapshot gained `zdir` / `rev`, and a reversal's `lvl`
  is its reversal level.
- **Pre-confirmation code.** It uses `zdir` for every zone test. Also new: reversal breakout step 5b,
  `TrackRev`, `ConfirmRev`, restoring the direction on `BREAK_FAIL`, and `contOff` when the BPR breaks.
- **Decisions.**
  - `TryTouch`, with its own skips (BAD_LEVEL / RR / COST, level 0).
  - `Decide` treats touch candidates like ready setups (one decision per bar, creation order).
  - The slot-owner exception now also covers a setup whose own touch position holds the slot.
- **Defaults are unchanged.** With CONTINUATION and no touch entry, records equal v5.

**Executor** (`BprFvgEA.mq5`, version 6.00).
- **Inputs:** `InpSetupType`, `InpTouchEntry`, `InpTouchTargetRR` (clamped 0.1–20).
- **Touch position.**
  - `ExecTouch` sends the market order with the same gates as a batch and a TP from the fill price.
  - `g_tp` tracks it; `ManageTouch` applies the time stop and 16:44 flat per the v4 switches.
  - `FinishTouch` writes a trades-CSV row with level `T`; `IsTracked` includes it.
- **Risk.**
  - Every entry uses `LevelBudget(g_nShares)`, where `g_nShares` = Fibonacci entries + 1.
  - A batch's daily-loss check adds an open touch position's planned risk.
- **Slot.** `SlotOwner` / `SlotFreeForDecision(id)` allow the touch owner's limits (hedging only). On netting, the
  touch position blocks the slot.
- **Visibility.**
  - Panel rules row and start/self-check lines: setup type, touch entry with its sent / not-sent counts.
  - Log texts for reversal breakouts and touch entries. A k = 0 skip no longer indexes `g_fib[-1]`.
  - Drawings: zone colour from `zdir`, and a "BUY/SELL MKT" marker.

**Harness and tests.**
- `tools/mql5_cpp_harness/bk_main.cpp`: keys `setup_type` / `touch_entry` / `touch_rr`; touch positions opened at the
  next bar's open (`H_TOUCH_*` lines); slot owner from `OccupyingId` / `PhaseOf`.
- `tests/bk_scenarios.py` `v6_sequences()`: 8 sequences, also in the parity set.
- `tests/test_bpr_breakout_v6_setups.py`: 52 tests.
