# Day 05 — 2026-10-08 — BPR + FVG Expert Advisor and its pre-registered test (EXP010)

## Request
Combine the LuxAlgo BPR and FVG features into one MQL5 EA. It must:
- draw the zones like the LuxAlgo indicator;
- use them to take buy and sell setups.

The owner had asked for the EA, so MQL5 work was approved for the Strategy Tester and demo accounts. Live trading is
**not** approved: real accounts are log-only.

## Work done
- **Specification.** `research/indicators/BPR_FVG_EA_SPEC.md` is the single source of truth. Its rules come from the
  owner's sketch and the H-13 playbook. The defaults were fixed before any data was looked at.
- **MQL5 EA** in `mql5/BprFvgEA/`: one main file plus 8 includes. The pure modules (`BfDefines`, `BfEngine`,
  `BfDetector`) hold the decision logic; the other modules handle execution, risk, sessions, drawing and logs.
  - **Not compiled**: MetaEditor is not available here.
- **Python reference** `qe/strategies/bpr_fvg.py`: a detector with the same decisions as the EA, plus a bar-based
  execution simulator. It has 63 tests.
- **Parity harness** `tests/test_bpr_fvg_ea_harness.py`. The pure EA modules are transliterated to C++ and compared
  event by event with the Python detector, across 10 configurations.
  - **First run:** 1 of 10 configurations failed, because of float rounding. The tick level 201734 × 0.01 is
    2017.3400000000001 in binary. On a bar whose high is exactly 2017.34, Python hit TP1 and the EA did not.
  - **Fix:** both ports now normalise levels to the symbol digits, like MT5's `NormalizeDouble`.
  - **Mutation checks:** single-character changes to the break, expiry, MSS and runaway comparisons were all caught.
  - The C++ shim gained `NormalizeDouble`, `MathFloor` and `MathCeil`.
- **EXP010.**
  - Pre-registered at `880ac1c` (`experiments/exp010_bpr_fvg_ea.py`).
  - A synthetic smoke test (`tests/test_exp010_smoke.py`) exercised the pipeline before the run.
  - Run in CI: research workflow run 37792966182.
- **Adversarial review of the EA.** Three auditors (compile, trading safety, conformance) plus skeptics produced 24
  findings. A fix agent applied them, and two more verifiers checked the diff.
  - The verifiers found no compile errors.
  - Five follow-ups were fixed by hand.
  - The same fail-open account guard was also fixed in `VideoStrategyEA` and `QuantumEdgeImpulseReversion`.

## Hypotheses
- **H-13 REJECTED as specified.** A limit order near the far edge of a BPR is adversely selected: of 483 placed orders,
  410 were cancelled because price ran to TP1 before filling, and 50 of the 62 fills hit the full stop.

## Mistakes caught and fixed
1. Python and the EA rounded levels to the tick in different ways (float representation). Found by the parity
   harness; both now normalise to the symbol digits.
2. Pending MARKET decisions were treated differently in the two ports. Found by the Python agent and fixed in the EA.
3. The EA review found execution-layer gaps:
   - the account guard failed open at terminal start;
   - stray positions could run past the 120-min limit;
   - the daily baseline could be captured before the account was ready;
   - pending orders were left behind on removal;
   - the partial close does not work on netting accounts;
   - a marketable limit ended the setup.

   All were fixed. The same account-guard flaw was in the two older EAs and was fixed there too.

## Limitations
- The EA has not been compiled in MetaEditor or run in MT5.
- The parity harness covers the decision logic only, not execution or drawing.
- EXP010 uses HistData bid bars with modelled spreads and an `UNVERIFIED` commission, and bar-based fills.
