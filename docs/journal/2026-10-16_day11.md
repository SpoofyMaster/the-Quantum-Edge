## What 7.01 did with it
The pattern itself (late T1, rejection, two closes below the T1 wick) was already the reversal. Three things stood in
the way:
- **The window.** The 240-bar setup window counted from the BPR's creation, so a first tap after 4 hours (M1) was lost
  (the setup was EXPIRED in WAIT).
- **The warm-up.** It dropped every BPR formed in the 5000 history bars at the start.
- **Speed.** By default the limits waited for a pullback candle (`InpAnticipateEntries = false`). The reversal also
  waited for an FVG of the drop after its 100 %. So the limits could come one or more bars after the second close.

## v8 (MQL5 only; the research defaults and the parity set unchanged)
**Detector.** `t1WaitBars` (EA `InpMaxBarsToT1`, EA default 0 = no limit; detector default −1 = v7 rules).
With ≥ 0:
- **Expiry before T1.** Only `t1WaitBars` applies before T1 (`Expired()`).
- **The window.** The breakout window counts from T1; a reversal setup counts from its last rejection when later.
- **Capacity.** At 128 setups the oldest setup still in WAIT makes room (`EvictOldestUntouched`, DONE(CAPACITY)).
- **Warm-up.** Setups in WAIT / ZONE / BREAK at the warm-up's end go on.

`revNeedFvg` (EA `InpRevNeedFvg`, EA default false; detector default true): the reversal's `FvgOk` is true, so with
`anticipate` its limits go in on the confirming close.

## Adversarial review of v8 (workflow: 3 reviewers + skeptics) and the fixes
**How it ran.** Three reviewers read the v8 diff: fidelity to the owner's request, the detector's state machine, and
the executor. The top two findings of each went to a skeptic, who tried to refute them with the C++ harness.
- **State machine:** no finding.
- **Executor:** two minor findings, both confirmed by the skeptics.
- **Fidelity:** three findings. The skeptics refuted the top two as "documented behaviour". I reversed one of those
  verdicts, and checked the third (not sent to a skeptic) by hand.

**Confirmed and fixed (executor).**
- **Warm-up setups were invisible.** The setups that go on after the warm-up had their T1, rejection and breakout in
  the warm-up. Those records are counted, not logged or drawn, so a setup could place orders on a T1 and a level that
  appeared neither on the chart nor in the log.
  - Fix: `ShowKeptSetups()` runs after the warm-up. It logs one line per kept setup
    (`#n REV SHORT kept from the warm-up: ZONE, touches 1, T1 <time>, level <price> (candle <time>)`), and draws its
    T1 / T2 labels and, in `BREAK`, the breakout line and `B1`.
- **Wrong text.** The start line, panel and self-check said "limits at the confirming close" whenever
  `InpRevNeedFvg = false`, even with `InpAnticipateEntries = false` (then the limits wait for a pullback candle).
  - Fix: `SetupTypeText` now reads both switches (and `InpFvgRule = NONE`). The input comment is reworded.

**Reversed refutation, implemented (detector).**
- **The window still ran while price was away.** After T1 and its rejection, the 240-bar window kept counting while
  price was away from the BPR. A late T2, or the late break of the T1 wick that the owner described ("finally price
  comes back again"), was lost: on the reviewer's bars the setup was EXPIRED on 260.
  - The skeptic: a documented default the owner can change with one input.
  - My verdict: the owner's words are about price going away and coming back, so the window must not run while price
    is away.
  - Fix (`Expired()`, with `t1WaitBars ≥ 0`): no expiry while the setup is in `ZONE` away from the zone (after a
    rejection). In a visit the window counts from the visit's first bar (`lastVisit`, new field), and in `BREAK` from
    the breakout candle.
  - Capacity follows the same idea (`EvictMostIdle`, renamed from `EvictOldestUntouched`). The setup idle the longest
    makes room: never touched (since its creation), or away from its zone (since its last visit or rejection). A
    setup in a visit, a breakout or a trade is never dropped.

**Checked by hand and implemented (detector).**
- **A one-candle reaction did not count as the rejection.** The owner's "bullish reaction after" T1 can be one candle
  that opens in the zone (T1 closed inside it) and closes above it. That candle belonged to the visit. If the next
  candle came back into the zone, the reversal never got its rejection and never armed.
  - Fix: with `t1WaitBars ≥ 0`, a reversal setup takes a candle inside a visit that closes beyond the zone's far edge
    as the `REJECT`, with all its effects (`rej`, `lastRej`, the level candidate, the leg candidate). A close exactly
    at the edge is not enough. A continuation setup keeps the v2 rule. `-1` keeps the v7 rule exactly.
  - In the owner's example (`tap_from_above`) the rejection is now on 18, not 19. The trade is unchanged. The chart
    is re-rendered with `R` on 18.

**Refuted and left as is (an open question for the owner).** With `BOTH`, the continuation's longs on the same BPR
can still be open when the reversal confirms: their stop is 10 ticks below the T1 wick. The reversal then waits for
the slot (one setup at a time) and can miss its first pullback. This is the documented one-setup-at-a-time rule, and
closing the other position would be a new trading rule, so it is the owner's decision (backlog U-20).
