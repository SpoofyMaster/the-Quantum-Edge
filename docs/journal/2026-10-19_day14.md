# Day 14 — 2026-10-19 — BprFvgEA v10: a time limit for the limit orders, and every confirmed setup traded

## Request (owner)
> "i need to set two more changes, first you have to set a limit time to configure on the input for how many time the
> EA should let the limit orders before canceling them, and second thing the EA should trade all the setups confirmed,
> for the moment when a setup is confirmed the EA place the limit orders but if on the same time another setup is also
> confirmed the EA skip it and continue monitoring and tracking the first trade"

## What was built (MQL5 only; the research defaults and the parity set unchanged)

### 1. The pending-order time limit
**Input.** `InpPendingMaxMin` (minutes, default 0 = off; detector `pendingMaxSec` = minutes × 60).

**Rule** (detector, spec §4.2–§4.4 note).
- **Start.** `ordTime` = `env.now` (the next bar's open: the decision moment) of the setup's **first** placement.
  A decision the executor answers with `NotifyRetry` (nothing sent) does not start it (`ordBar` = the bar of that
  first placement; `NotifyRetry` resets both when it answers that decision).
- **The check.** `timeUp` = `env.now − ordTime ≥ pendingMaxSec`, on every bar close.
  - `ORDERED`: cancel the pending levels (`CANCEL EXPIRED`), `DONE(EXPIRED)`.
  - `FILLED`: cancel the pending levels; the filled ones run on.
  - `LEG` after a move that was not sent again: `DONE(EXPIRED)`.
- **Moves.** A re-anchor never resets `ordTime`.
- **Records.** Records and cancel intents carry `pendTime`.

**Executor.**
- `env.now = nextOpen`.
- **Broker-side expiry.** Each limit's expiry is also capped at `ordTime + limit + one bar`, at least one minute
  ahead.
- **Log.** The cancel lines read `EXPIRED: pending-order time limit, N min after the first placement`.
- **Panel.** The `ended:` row counts these setups; the rules row shows the limit.

### 2. Several setups at a time
**Input.** `InpMaxSetups` (default 5, 1..10; detector `maxSetups`; 1 = the v2-v9 rule).

**Detector** (`DecideMulti`, spec §5).
- **Gates.** Session, risk and spread, as before.
- **Order.** Every ready setup decides in creation order while `env.freeSlots` lasts.
- **Own slot.** A setup that re-anchored on this bar (`reAnchorBar == u`), or whose own touch position is open
  (`env.slotOwner`), decides without a free slot.
- **Touch entry.** One at a time, and it needs a free slot.

**Executor.**
- **Contexts.** One execution context per setup: `g_cx[10]`, with the macro `g_ctx` = `g_cx[g_ci]`.
  - `SyncTrade` / `ManagePositions` loop over the contexts.
  - `ExecCancel` / `ExecBatch` select the setup's context.
  - `IsTracked` / `IsTrackedOrder` look in all of them.
  - `ExecuteIntents` runs one batch per deciding setup.
- **Slots.** `MaxSetupsNow()` = the input on hedging, 1 on netting. `UsedSlots()` = busy contexts + a touch position
  whose setup has none. `BuildEnv` gives `env.freeSlots` (0 while an unowned or untracked position / order exists).
- **A batch** (`MultiSlotWait`).
  - Its own context when it re-anchored on this bar; otherwise a free slot and an unused context.
  - **Risk.** The daily-loss check adds the planned risk of every other setup's pending and open levels
    (`PlannedOther`), so all setups together stay within the 1.00 % planned daily loss. Each setup keeps its 0.20 %.
- **Texts.** The panel lists every setup's orders / positions (`2 of 5 setups`). The self-check explains the slots and
  the daily cap (netting: 1).
- **Version.** CSV files `BFEA10_*`; version 10.00.

**Detector capacity.** `BF_MAX_INTENTS` raised from 32 to 128. A random series with 10 slots lost 17 intents in one
bar: several setups moving their orders at the same new extreme (3 cancels + 3 orders each).

**Harness.**
- New keys: `max_setups`, `pend_max_sec`, `bar_sec`; `fills` (`H_FILL` lines); `slots` (one `H_SLOTS` line per bar:
  busy setups, free slots, `BLOCKED_SLOT` of the bar, busy after, touch owner); `retry_bar` (the executor answers
  that bar's decisions with `NotifyRetry`).
- `ext` records carry `pt`. stderr prints `MAXBUSY`.
- Multi mode: `env.freeSlots` = `maxSetups` − `BusyCount()` − a touch position whose setup is not busy.

## Adversarial review (workflow: 3 reviewers + skeptics)
- **First run** (on commit 7e8322e): the three reviewers (executor, detector / harness / tests, fidelity / safety)
  all stopped on a usage limit before reporting. Their scratch files were read; the detector reviewer's own mutation
  set gave the three test gaps above. It had also checked, with its own scripts, that v10 at the 9.01 settings gives
  records identical to 9.01 (65 hand-built sequences long / mirrored × 6 configurations; 16 random series × 7
  configurations): 0 differences, the new `pt` field aside.
- **Second run** (on commit 48b4a8e): 15 findings, the top 2 of each reviewer sent to a skeptic. **6 confirmed** (1
  major, 5 minor), none refuted; the other 9 were not sent to a skeptic and were checked by reading. All fixed in the
  next commit, except where noted.

**Confirmed and fixed.**
- **Copies of one trade** (fidelity; claimed critical, the skeptic: major).
  - Stacked BPRs often give their setups the same leg (same 100 %, same 0 %), above all reversal setups. Each sent
    the same three limits with the same stop.
  - Measured on 40 random series with `REVERSAL` and 5 slots: 4826 of 20261 decisions came in identical groups (up to
    5 copies), and 2294 fills were duplicates. So one trade could carry up to 1.00 %. None with one setup at a time,
    none with `CONTINUATION`.
  - **Fix: one trade per leg.** In `DecideMulti`, a ready setup whose trade direction and leg origin (so its stop)
    equal those of a setup with orders / positions ends `DONE(NO_LEVEL)`, with `dupOf` naming that setup.
  - No new reason code: the reason list is shared with the Python reference's stats layout.
  - EA log `DONE (NO_LEVEL: the same leg - direction and 100 % - is already traded by #n; one trade per leg)`, panel
    count.
  - Test `test_one_trade_per_leg`; `_check_cap` now asserts that no two setups hold live orders with the same
    direction and stop.
- **A setup whose own context is still busy took a second slot** (executor, minor).
  - Case: its old order could not be deleted, or filled just before its cancel. The context already counts as used.
    In `LEG` with anticipation, the setup decided again on every bar, took one more unit of `free_`, and was refused
    by the EA every time, while a newer setup was kept out.
  - Fix: the EA lists its busy contexts in `env.busyIds`; `DecideMulti` skips such a setup without taking a slot,
    except on its re-anchor bar. The wait text now tells an open position from a failed delete.
  - Harness `ghost_bars` (the first move of each setup leaves its context busy for N bars) and test
    `test_a_setup_whose_context_is_busy_takes_no_other_slot`. Without the fix: 31-469 bars per run where a setup was
    kept out while a slot was free.
- **The deleted-order list was too small** (executor; claimed major, the skeptic: minor).
  - 16 entries, sized for one setup. With 6-10 setups, one new extreme can delete 18-30 orders on one bar.
  - The oldest unproven entries were then dropped, which switched off the fill-free proof for them.
  - Fix: 48 entries (`BF_MAX_DEL`); entries older than one minute go first.
- **Test gaps** (detector, minor).
  - No multi-setup case without anticipation filled the slots: a mutant `reAnchorBar >= 0` broke the cap and passed.
    Fix: two such cases in `MULTI`.
  - The three move / retry / kept-orders time-limit tests now also run with 5 slots.
  - The `BLOCKED_*` counters in multi mode were never asserted. Fix: with 2 slots the reversal series must fill them
    (`BLOCKED_SLOT > 0`); each gate test checks its own counter and that the other two stay 0.
  - The docstring "the busy count reaches cap" was corrected.
- **"Nothing is ever added to a losing trade"** (fidelity; claimed major, the skeptic: minor).
  - The skeptic: the reviewer's example was a resting L2 of a pre-planned ladder, not a new decision during a loss.
    No order is triggered or sized from a loss, and the owner asked for every confirmed setup to trade.
  - What is true: a new setup's decision can come while another setup's position in the same direction is in loss
    (3 of 3061 batches with `CONTINUATION`, 321 of 23271 with `BOTH`).
  - Fix: README wording only. Same-direction setups can trade while an earlier one is in loss, each from its own
    signal with its own stop and 0.20 %. The 1.00 % holds at the planned stops; several stops slipping at once can
    exceed it. `InpMaxSetups = 1` gives one trade at a time.

**Not sent to a skeptic, checked and fixed.**
- **The time limit lagged up to one bar above M1**, and the broker-side expiry came one bar after the limit. Fix: the
  broker-side expiry is now exactly the first placement + the limit, so the broker ends the order to the minute; the
  EA's cancel follows on the bar close. The log names a broker-side expiry.
- **`InpMaxTradesDay` ignored the other setups' pending orders.** Fix: a batch waits when today's positions + the other
  contexts' pending levels reach it. One setup at a time is unchanged.
- **A market fill whose position id is unknown took the EA's first position.** With several positions that can be
  another setup's. Fix: `UntrackedPosition(dir, lots)` takes the untracked position of its direction and volume (an
  orphan that matches is taken back), for the levels and for the touch position.
- **README: a slot-blocked setup does not log** `slot busy: all N setup slots are busy`; the detector holds it back
  before any order. Corrected: the panel counts it.
- **`test_random_series_time_limit`** relied on records sorted by bar (the harness prints a bar's fills before the
  exits checked on it). Fix: sorted.
- **`test_explicit_defaults_equal_default`** claimed "= v9" without checking it. Docstring corrected. The check was
  done once instead: a 9.01 build (from commit 4973a33) against v10 at its defaults gives **0 differences in 1006
  runs** (65 hand-built sequences, long and mirrored, × 7 configurations incl. touch entries, keep orders and session
  cancels; 16 random series × 6 configurations), the new `pt` / `dup` fields aside.
- **On M1, `InpPendingMaxMin` ≥ 60 does nothing** with the default `InpLegExpiryBars = 60`: the leg expiry comes
  first. Fix: a self-check note, and the README says so.

## Limitations
- **Not compiled in MetaEditor. No MT5 run.**
  - The executor is the largest change since v6: one context per setup, the `g_ctx` macro, the slot and daily-loss
    checks.
  - It was checked by reading and the static check. An adversarial review (3 reviewers + skeptics) was running at
    this commit; its results and fixes follow below in a later commit.
- **No backtest.** H-14 / H-15 stay `HYPOTHESIS`. A run with `InpMaxSetups > 1` or `InpPendingMaxMin > 0` is a new
  trial (hypotheses register).
- **Netting accounts** stay at one setup at a time.
- **The time limit is checked on bar closes.** The broker-side expiry, one bar later, is the safety net.
