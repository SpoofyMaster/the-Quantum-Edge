## What v8 did
A reversal needed one `REJECT` after T1: one candle that leaves the zone, or (v8) one candle that closes back above
it. So T1, one green reaction candle, then two closes below the T1 wick was a reversal. Two v8 test sequences are
exactly that (`late_t1`, `one_candle_reaction`).

## v9 (MQL5 only; the research defaults and the parity set unchanged)
**Detector.** `revRejCandles` (EA `InpRevRejCandles`, EA default 2; detector default 0 = v8).
- **The count.** After the T1 candle, a candle in the BPR's direction (green for a bullish BPR, red for a bearish
  one) that does not close beyond the reversal level is a rejection candle (`nRejC`). The candles need not follow each
  other.
- **What does not count.** The T1 candle itself, a doji, and a candle that closes beyond the level (price is already
  through the BPR).
- **Price coming back too early.** A close beyond the level restarts the count from the next candle (flag
  `rejCReset`): "they must be ... before price comes back for breakout". This also applies after a failed breakout.
- **The breakout.** Step 5b also needs `nRejC ≥ revRejCandles`. The `REJECT` is still needed
  (`InpRevNeedRejection`). Continuation setups are not affected.
- **Records.** Each record carries the count (`rejC`).

**Executor (9.00).**
- The input `InpRevRejCandles` (0..100).
- Log text: a reversal `REJECT` shows `rejection candles since T1: n; the breakout needs N`; a reversal `BREAKOUT`
  shows `n rejection candles before it`; the warm-up line shows the count.
- The rules row, version strings, `BFEA9_*` CSV files.

**Harness.** Key `rev_rej_c`. With `ext=1` it prints `rc`.
