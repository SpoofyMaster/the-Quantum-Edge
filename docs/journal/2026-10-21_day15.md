## The rule as implemented (MQL5 only; research defaults and parity set unchanged)
**Inputs.** `InpRevBodyInBox` (EA default true); detector `revBodyInBox` (default false = v10).
- **Out of the box.** For a reversal setup on a bullish BPR (tapped from above), a body (open and close) is out when
  it goes below the box's bottom: `min(open, close) < B`. A bearish BPR is the mirror image: `max(open, close) > T`.
  On the edge is in the box. The wicks are free.
- **T1 / T2.** The touch candle, and every next candle of the same touch: consecutive candles touching the box are
  one touch. A flag `tVisit` marks a touch visit: set at a touch, cleared at its rejection, at a pullback visit and
  at a breakout.
- **C1 .. C`InpRevRejCandles`.** The rejection candles the breakout needs (C1, C2 with the default 2). A third one is
  not checked: "C1 and C2". After an early close the count restarts, and the new C1, C2 are checked.
- **Early second tap (review fix, below).** A visit opened by a v9 early close (beyond the reversal level after fewer
  than N C) is not a touch since v9. While a second touch is allowed, it is the owner's T2 and is checked as T2.
- **Out = no trade.** The setup ends on that close: `DONE(BROKEN)` with `bodyOut`. It happens before the v6 touch
  entry, so no touch entry follows.
  - Codes: n = Tn, 10 + n = Cn, 1000 + n = a later candle of the Tn touch.
  - No new reason code: the reason list is shared with the Python reference's stats layout.
  - EA log `DONE (BROKEN: the body of T1 (open …, close …) is below the BPR box (bottom …) - no trade)`.
  - Chart label `NO TRADE: T1 body out of the BPR` (`T1-touch candle` for a later candle). It sits on the other side
    of the candle from the T label.
  - Panel `ended: broken n (body out of the box m)`, warm-up included.
  - Self-check note; version 11.00; `BFEA11_*` CSV files.
- **Not checked** (the owner's words name only T and C):
  - pullback visits after the last allowed touch;
  - breakout candles, and the candles after a failed breakout in the same visit;
  - C candles after C`N`.

  A red candle coming back into the box while a touch is allowed is T2 (or the early T2), so it **is** checked.
  Continuation setups already end `BROKEN` at a wick beyond the box.

**Harness.** Key `rev_body_box`. With `ext=1` it prints `bo` and the box `zb` / `zt`.

**Chart (first deliverable).** `tools/bk_preview/draw_body_rule.py` writes
`research/indicators/preview/bpr_body_rule_example.svg` (PNG sent to the owner). Four panels from the v11 detector's
records:
1. a valid case (T1 wick below the box, body in it; C1 in the box, C2 above it): trades;
2. the owner's "T1 NO" (T1 closes at 97.80 < 98.00): no trade, while v10 placed limits on 23;
3. C2 opens and closes below the box: no trade, while v10 placed limits on 25. Since the review, the panel notes
   that it is drawn with a gap (C2 opens 0.60 below the close before it). Without the gap, that red candle closes
   below the box and ends the setup as a later candle of the T1 touch;
4. the mirror image (tapped from below): T1 closes above the box, no trade.

**Choices where the words leave room.**
- **Every candle of the touch,** not only the first: he wrote "T1 Candles", and the detector counts consecutive
  touching candles as one touch.
- **The whole body for C,** not only the close: "above or on the box".
- **Only C1 .. C`N`:** "C1 and C2".

## Adversarial review (same day; workflow, 3 reviewers + skeptics)
12 findings: 1 major, 11 minor; one appears twice (the tapped-from-below claims).
- **Skeptics:** 4 runs; 3 findings confirmed, 1 refuted (a design choice, now documented).
- **Cut by the session limit:** 2 skeptic runs. I re-checked both findings on the harness: both confirmed.
- **Not sent to skeptics:** 6 findings. I checked them by reading and on the harness, and fixed them.
- **Major (fixed): an early second tap was never checked.** BPR [98.00, 99.90]:
  - T1 (17) has its wick in the box at 99.0, and C1 (18) closes above the box;
  - 19 comes back and closes at 97.80, below the box. That is below the T1 wick after one green candle, so v9 made it
    a pullback visit (no touch);
  - its body was never checked, and the setup traded (limits on 26, same as v10).

  When the T1 wick is in the box, a close below the box is always beyond the level, so "T2 body out" could only
  fire with a T1 wick below the box. Fix: while a second touch is allowed, that visit is checked as T2 (`tVisitK`).
  The v9 state is unchanged. Sequences `early_t2`, `early_t2_next_out`.
- **Later candles of a touch** were reported as "T1" with their own open / close. They now have the code 1000 + n:
  `a later candle of the T1 touch`, chart `T1-touch candle`. 20 + n would collide with C codes up to 10 + 100.
- **Warm-up body-outs** were missing from the panel's `body out of the box` count, while `broken` counts the warm-up.
  `CountWarmEvents` now counts them.
- **The log said "closed below"** when only the open was out (every out C candle). It now says "is below / above the
  BPR box".
- **The NO TRADE label** sat on top of the T label of the same candle. It is now on the other side of the candle.
- **The self-check note and the setup text** said "C1, C2, ..." whatever `InpRevRejCandles` is. They are now built
  from it ("C1, C2"; no C at 0).
- **README / journal "not checked: a red candle after the rejection"** was wrong: with 2 touches that candle is T2. The
  not-checked list now names the real cases.
- **The tapped-from-below claims** ("v10 traded them", "still trade") were not tested: another BPR held the slot. The
  tests now use `below()`.
- **Chart panel 3** (C2 below the box) needs a 0.60 gap that M1 data rarely has. The panel and the README now say so.
- **README CSV note** mapped five file prefixes to four versions; fixed.
- **Refuted (documented):** the candles after a failed breakout in the same visit are not checked. The skeptic: they
  are neither T nor C, they belong to the breakout move, and checking them would add a condition the owner did not
  state. Listed under "not checked".

## Limitations
- **Not compiled in MetaEditor. No MT5 run.** The executor's changes (input, log text, chart label, counter) were
  checked by reading and the static check.
- **No backtest.** H-15 stays `HYPOTHESIS`; v11 is a new rule for it.
- **Candles that are neither T nor C are not checked:**
  - a pullback visit after the last allowed touch;
  - the breakout candles, and the candles after a failed breakout;
  - C candles after C`N`.

  The owner's words name only T1 / T2 and C1 / C2. If he wants every candle between T1 and the breakout to stay in the
  box, that is a small change.
- **On M1 without gaps,** a C body below the box mostly needs the candle before it to close below the box. If that
  candle is a touch candle it already ends the setup, so the C check matters mainly after a gap or after an unchecked
  candle.
