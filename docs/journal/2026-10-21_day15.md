## The rule as implemented (MQL5 only; research defaults and parity set unchanged)
**Inputs.** `InpRevBodyInBox` (EA default true); detector `revBodyInBox` (default false = v10).
- **Out of the box.** For a reversal setup on a bullish BPR (tapped from above), a body (open and close) is out when
  it goes below the box's bottom: `min(open, close) < B`. A bearish BPR is the mirror image: `max(open, close) > T`.
  On the edge is in the box. The wicks are free.
- **T1 / T2.** The touch candle, and every next candle of the same touch: consecutive candles touching the box are
  one touch. A flag `tVisit` marks a touch visit: set at a touch, cleared at its rejection, at a pullback visit and
  at a breakout.
- **C1 .. C`InpRevRejCandles`.** The rejection candles the breakout needs (C1, C2 with the default 2). A third one is
  not checked: "C1 and C2". After an early close the count restarts, and the new C1, C2 are checked.
- **Out = no trade.** The setup ends on that close: `DONE(BROKEN)` with `bodyOut` (n = Tn, 10 + n = Cn). It happens
  before the v6 touch entry, so no touch entry follows.
  - No new reason code: the reason list is shared with the Python reference's stats layout.
  - EA log `DONE (BROKEN: the body of T1 (open …, close …) closed below the BPR box (bottom …) - no trade)`.
  - Chart label `NO TRADE: T1 body out of the BPR` on the candle; panel `ended: broken n (body out of the box m)`.
  - Self-check note; version 11.00; `BFEA11_*` CSV files.
- **Not checked** (the owner's words name only T and C):
  - other candles, such as a red candle after the rejection;
  - pullback visits after the last allowed touch;
  - breakout candles.

  Continuation setups already end `BROKEN` at a wick beyond the box.

**Harness.** Key `rev_body_box`. With `ext=1` it prints `bo` and the box `zb` / `zt`.

**Chart (first deliverable).** `tools/bk_preview/draw_body_rule.py` writes
`research/indicators/preview/bpr_body_rule_example.svg` (PNG sent to the owner). Four panels from the v11 detector's
records:
1. a valid case (T1 wick below the box, body in it; C1 in the box, C2 above it): trades;
2. the owner's "T1 NO" (T1 closes at 97.80 < 98.00): no trade, while v10 placed limits on 23;
3. C2 opens and closes below the box: no trade, while v10 placed limits on 25;
4. the mirror image (tapped from below): T1 closes above the box, no trade.

**Choices where the words leave room.**
- **Every candle of the touch,** not only the first: he wrote "T1 Candles", and the detector counts consecutive
  touching candles as one touch.
- **The whole body for C,** not only the close: "above or on the box".
- **Only C1 .. C`N`:** "C1 and C2".
