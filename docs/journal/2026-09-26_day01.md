## Hypotheses accepted / rejected
- VERIFIED (synthetic): M-01 (null specificity), M-02 (no directional leakage), M-03 (AUC inflated
  by cost structure; directional models must beat a cost/regime-only baseline).
- PARTIAL: M-04 (power). An unconditional, all-bars logistic model cannot see a rare, event-triggered
  edge. **Decision:** probability models will be fit on event-conditioned samples (BACKLOG P-1).
- VERIFIED (arithmetic): M-05. Costs dominate below roughly 8–10 pip-equivalent stops. **Design
  implication:** prefer setups with stop distances ≥ 8–12 pips (FX) or ≥ $1.20 (XAUUSD), meaning the
  15–120 minute part of the horizon range, not 5-minute scalps. Cost-in-R becomes an explicit
  feature and filter.
- Market hypotheses H-01…H-10: **BLOCKED** (no data).
