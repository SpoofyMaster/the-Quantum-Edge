# Day 06 — 2026-10-09 — "The EA does not place limit orders" + the owner's BPR breakout / Fibonacci setup (v2)

## Request (owner)
1. "I have tested the EA on MT5 and the problem is the EA is not placing limit orders, so please fix this first."
2. A new setup rule, quoted verbatim in `research/indicators/BPR_BREAKOUT_FIB_SPEC.md` §0:
   - a BPR is used once and touched at most twice, with a rejection between the touches;
   - a breakout of the high or low of the last candle before the rejection, with at least one FVG;
   - two candle-body closes beyond that level;
   - the EA follows the breakout leg and detects the pullback;
   - a Fibonacci from the leg origin to the leg extreme, with entries at 50 / 61 / 71 % and the target at the leg
     extreme.

## 1. Diagnosis: why v1 placed (almost) no limit orders
No MT5 log was available, so the diagnosis comes from reading the code (`mql5/archive/BprFvgEA_v1_H13`):
- **Silent filters.**
  - The detector decided an order only if round-trip cost ≤ **0.15 R** and reward:risk at TP1 ≥ **1.0**.
  - A failed check returned without any log line, so the setup stayed `ARMED` until it broke or expired.
  - On M1 the zones are usually too small for the cost check: on XAUUSD the stop had to be ≳ 1.3–2.6 price units, on
    EURUSD ≳ 7–13 pips.
- **Already visible in EXP010.** The backtest placed only about 7 orders a month on XAUUSD (483 in 66 months); 7,146
  setups expired. On M1 FX the count would be close to zero.
- **No compile-level or API bug was found in the order path:** account guard, warm-up, `DayCheck`, `PlaceLimit`. Two
  execution weaknesses were fixed in v2:
  - the symbol's FOK/IOC filling mode was sent on pending orders, with a single retry on `INVALID_FILL`;
  - an expiration the server refused was not retried.
- **Unconfirmed causes.** Without the owner's log, an "Algo Trading" switch that is off, or a real account (log-only
  by design), cannot be excluded. v2 now reports both.

## 4. Mistakes caught and fixed during the session
1. **Invalid hand-built bars.** Several bars had open or close outside high/low. They are now refused by
   `bk_scenarios.check()`.
2. **The LuxAlgo-negative test was wrong.** It assumed the base scenario had no LuxAlgo FVG; the engine does make one
   on bar 18. A dedicated `no_lux_fvg` scenario replaces it.
3. **The first causality test was too strict.** A `Notify*` record carries `n` = the last processed bar, as the spec
   says. The test now checks the prefix property instead.
4. **A latent sizing bug.** With an unknown tick value, `LossPerLot` returned 0 and the size came from the commission
   alone. v2 refuses to size in that case. v1 is frozen and had the same latent bug; its warm-up requires a tick
   value first.

### Fixed (confirmed)
| # | Finding | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Breaker (high).** One decision with 3 refused orders tripped the order breaker until the next FX day, and the panel reported it as "risk". This could explain "no limit orders" on demo. | One strike per decision. Transient and permission retcodes are not counted. Auto-reset after 30 min. Reported as `BREAKER TRIPPED` / last wait, no longer folded into `riskOk`. |
| 2 | **Filling (unsure → fixed anyway).** `CTrade::FillingCheck` overrides the filling mode of pending orders, so "RETURN first" never took effect and the self-check text was wrong. | `CTrade` first; on `INVALID_FILL` a raw `OrderSend` with RETURN. The filling actually sent is shown. |
| 3 | **Daily loss** missed manual closes of EA positions (exit deals with magic 0). | Two-pass accounting by position id. |
| 4 | **Partial fill.** A failed delete of the remainder was never retried, and the filled part had no time stop. | Throttled retry. The time stop and the 16:44 flat apply to the partial position. |
| 5 | **Removal.** Removing the EA left positions open with no time stop. | They are closed, by a raw request, on remove / chart close / terminal close / template / program stop. |
| 6 | **Netting.** No risk check after a market fill. | The whole position is closed when its risk exceeds the levels' budgets. |
| 7 | **Retries.** Failed deletes and closes were re-sent every tick, and CTrade printed each failure. | 3-s throttle; management paused (logged once) while algo trading is off; CTrade logging off. |
| 8 | **Rollover.** With the session filter off, orders were allowed at 16:44–17:00 New York. | Guard in the executor, and pending orders deleted in that window. |
| 9 | **Reconnect.** "Account not confirmed" (a reconnect) on demo killed the setup. | `NotifyRetry`. |
| 10 | **Rule (conformance F1).** A candle still touching the zone could be the breakout candle. | It is now part of the touch (new reference). Spec revision 2; Python, MQL5 and harness updated. Optional strict reading `InpRejectBeforeBreak` (default off). |
| 11 | **Drawings.** Order lines were missing when a decision was not sent, and the setup zone was not drawn. | Fib drawn before execution; DROP labels; a setup zone outline in the trade colour. |
| 12 | **Diagnostics.** | "Entry decisions" next to "orders accepted"; DONE records carry the phase they ended in; expired split into before / after the breakout; every DONE reason counted. |
