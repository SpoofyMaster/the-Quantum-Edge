## v7 rules (MQL5 only, like v5 / v6)
**Detector** (`BfDefines.mqh`, `BfDetector.mqh`).
- **Level.** `revLevel` (`InpRevLevel`) chooses the reversal level:
  - `DEEPEST` (default): the deeper wick of the T1 / T2 candles, the T1 wick in the example;
  - `T1`;
  - `LAST` (v6).
- **Rejection first.** `revNeedReject` (`InpRevNeedRejection`, default on) needs a `REJECT` after T1 before the
  reversal breakout.
- **Pullback visits.** For a reversal setup, a visit of the zone after the last allowed touch is the pullback. It is
  not counted, does not end the setup and does not move the level.
- **The 100 %.** It is the highest high since T1. A later visit starts a candidate (`cO` / `cX`). If the visit is
  rejected, the candidate becomes the leg, so the 100 % is the top of the last bounce. A visit that turns into the
  breakout keeps the earlier top.
- **BOTH = two setups per BPR.** A continuation setup and a reversal setup (`kind`), each on its own with its own id
  and records. The continuation one runs the v5 rules unchanged. The touch entry belongs to the continuation setup
  (REVERSAL: to the reversal setup).
- **Removed.** The v6 one-setup hand-over (`wasRev`, `contOff`).
- **Defaults.** CONTINUATION is unchanged (parity set).

**Executor** (`BprFvgEA.mq5` 7.00).
- The inputs `InpRevLevel` and `InpRevNeedRejection`.
- `#n REV` in the log, and the level option in the breakout line.
- `BFEA7_*` CSV files (v6 columns); `setup` = the setup's kind.
- Self-check and start lines.

**Harness.**
- Keys `rev_level`, `rev_reject`.
- `ext=1` prints `kind` / `zdir` / `lvl` / `lvlBar` (never in the parity runs).

**Chart.** `tools/bk_preview/draw_reversal.py` writes `research/indicators/preview/bpr_reversal_example.svg`. It
